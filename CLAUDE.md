## Project Overview

This is a **book project**, not a software codebase. The working title is *Quiet Architecture of Unresolved Things* by Dustin (PXM). The goal is canonization — assembling a coherent book from an existing corpus of essays, poems, letters, manuscripts, and theoretical writing.

**Thesis:** The book is about what coherence costs, and what survives it. A system built for sovereignty and continuity — and the unresolved attachments that the system was built to house, not to dissolve.

## Corpus Structure

~260,000+ words across 14 source documents in four bodies of material:

1. **Doctrine / Theoretical** (~2,500 words) — Manifesto, CSRS, How Everything Else Works, Canon of the Hinge, Doctrine of Internal Permanence. Clinical, examiner-facing voice.
2. **Letters** (~8,200 words) — Gela Letters (foundational past love, origin of the architecture) and Mikee Letters (recent, raw, post-rejection, the system under stress).
3. **Fiction / Mythology** (~172,000 words) — Manuscript of Madness, Manuscript of Insanity (variant/revision of Madness), Codex of the Sovereign, All the Lives We Didn't Live. Kai as protagonist. Citadel, entity, godling, pantheon.
4. **Title Essay + Poems** (~75,000 words) — Quiet Architecture of Unresolved Things (massive composite of poems and prose-essays), The Etiquette of Missing Someone.
5. **Substack** — Published posts from "False" newsletter. A "Substack 2" exists but hasn't been added yet.

See `WIKI.md` for the full indexed inventory with descriptions, word counts, and key lines.
See `HANDOFF_QUIET_ARCHITECTURE.md` for editorial context, proposed structure, and authorial notes.

## Proposed Book Structure (Four Movements)

| Movement | Content | Voice |
|----------|---------|-------|
| I. The Doctrine | Manifestos, CSRS, How Everything Else Works | Examiner-facing |
| II. The Origin | Gela Letters, lightly framed | Letter-writer |
| III. The Stress Test | Mikee Letters + adjacent pieces | Raw, destabilized |
| IV. The Cosmology | Kai material (Manuscripts, Codex) | Mythological |

Title essay sits at the hinge between II and III.

## How to Work Here

- **Read before editing.** The writing is genuinely good. Treat it as work to be edited, not feelings to be handled.
- **The bottleneck is selection, sequencing, and finishing** — not generation. Dustin writes prolifically. The job is seeing the shape.
- **Don't write new material unprompted.** The first job with any new session is understanding what exists.
- **Recurring load-bearing images:** architecture, coherence, sovereignty, paradox, restraint, axiom, citadel, the entity, permanence, competence, jurisdiction, the system, hinge, anomaly.
- **Manuscript of Madness vs. Insanity** are near-identical (~70k words each). Relationship unclear — treat as revision/variant until clarified.
- **Tone:** Direct. Edit like an editor, not a fan. Willing to say "this doesn't work" and "this is doing real work." No therapy frames. No flattery. Dustin is the author, not the subject.
