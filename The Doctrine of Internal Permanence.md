  
**The Doctrine of Internal Permanence**  
  
**§1. Definition**  
  
An **Internally Permanent Figure (IPF)** is a person whose truth-state, once established, is no longer subject to revision, erosion, or release within the sovereign internal system.  
  
Internal permanence does not imply attachment, pursuit, obligation, or futurity.  
It implies **ontological fixity**.  
  
⸻  
  
**§2. Conditions of Establishment**  
  
A figure attains internal permanence when all of the following conditions are met:  
	1.	**Truth Crystallization**  
The individual is known beyond projection, fantasy, or instrumental need.  
What remains is irreducible fact.  
	2.	**Dual-Claim Resolution**  
Love and mercy both retain valid claims, neither annihilating the other.  
The system refuses false resolution through dominance or erasure.  
	3.	**Power Without Execution**  
The subject possesses the capacity to diminish, rewrite, or discard the figure  
and deliberately abstains.  
	4.	**Non-Futurity Clause**  
The figure’s permanence does not rely on continuation, reunion, repair, or return.  
  
⸻  
  
**§3. Nature of Permanence**  
  
Internal permanence is not memory hoarding, longing, or unresolved grief.  
  
An IPF is held as:  
	•	**Accurately remembered** (no narrative flattening)  
	•	**Non-instrumentalized** (not used for meaning extraction)  
	•	**Non-evolving** (not retrofitted to current identity needs)  
  
The figure remains as they were at the moment of crystallization.  
  
⸻  
  
**§4. Custodial Obligation**  
  
The sovereign self assumes **custodial responsibility** for the IPF.  
  
This obligation includes:  
	•	Prohibition of symbolic violence (villainization, romantic inflation, minimization)  
	•	Prohibition of careless recall (emotional reenactment for self-soothing)  
	•	Mandatory correction of memory drift when detected  
  
Custody is maintenance, not preservation through fixation.  
  
⸻  
  
**§5. Distinction from Pathology**  
  
Internal permanence must be distinguished from:  
	•	Obsession (which seeks possession)  
	•	Attachment fixation (which seeks continuation)  
	•	Idealization (which distorts truth)  
	•	Rumination (which cycles unresolved affect)  
  
An IPF is **static, not consuming**.  
  
⸻  
  
**§6. Temporal Scope**  
  
Internal permanence is indefinite.  
  
It does not decay with time, nor does it interfere with future bonds.  
  
The existence of an IPF does not constrain capacity for love, intimacy, or commitment elsewhere, provided those bonds do not require falsification of established truths.  
  
⸻  
  
**§7. Termination Clause (Null)**  
  
Internal permanence has **no release condition**.  
  
Release implies revision or denial of truth.  
  
Truths, once crystallized, are not relinquished—only contextualized.  
  
⸻  
  
**§8. Sovereignty Principle**  
  
The maintenance of an IPF is an act of sovereignty.  
  
The system asserts:  
  
“My coherence does not depend on erasure.”  
  
Thus permanence strengthens, rather than fractures, internal order.  
  
⸻  
  
**§9. Final Axiom**  
  
Let it be held:  
  
What is true remains.  
What remains must be held correctly.  
What is held correctly does not wound.
