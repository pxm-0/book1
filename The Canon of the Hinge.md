  
**The Canon of the Hinge**  
  
**I. On Position**  
  
I occupy a position **after formation and before recommitment**.  
Affect has already performed its developmental function; it no longer governs.  
What remains is not emptiness, but **structure**.  
  
I am not waiting to feel again.  
I am waiting to decide **what justifies change when urgency is absent**.  
  
⸻  
  
**II. On Affect**  
  
Affect is neither denied nor privileged.  
It is acknowledged as historically formative and presently intermittent.  
  
When affect arises, it is received without panic.  
When it does not, absence is not interpreted as loss.  
  
I do not measure vitality by intensity.  
  
⸻  
  
**III. On Sovereignty**  
  
Sovereignty is not a defense; it is an **ethical stance**.  
  
I reserve final authority over self-reorganization.  
No experience, relationship, or desire is granted automatic jurisdiction.  
  
Change is permitted, but not conscripted.  
  
⸻  
  
**IV. On Refusal**  
  
Refusal is not pathology.  
  
I refuse:  
	•	Transformations that dissolve coherence  
	•	Attachments that demand surrender without justification  
	•	Urgencies that cannot explain themselves  
  
Refusal is an action, not a failure of action.  
  
⸻  
  
**V. On Motion**  
  
Stasis is not my aim.  
  
Motion continues through:  
	•	Meaning  
	•	Competence  
	•	Ethical alignment  
	•	Deliberate engagement  
  
I move without needing to be pulled.  
  
⸻  
  
**VI. On Renewal**  
  
Renewal is not owed to emotion.  
  
For renewal to be legitimate, it must:  
	1.	Introduce new information  
	2.	Preserve internal authority  
	3.	Increase coherence rather than fracture it  
  
Intensity alone is insufficient evidence.  
  
⸻  
  
**VII. On the Present Tension**  
  
I acknowledge the hinge.  
  
I am not undecided because I am afraid.  
I am undecided because **the threshold is high**.  
  
The question is not *whether* to change,  
but **what earns the right to change me**.  
  
⸻  
  
**VIII. On Patience**  
  
Nothing is broken by waiting.  
  
This pause is not avoidance;  
it is **discernment under conditions of sufficiency**.  
  
I will not fabricate hunger to justify motion.  
  
⸻  
  
**Closing Clause**  
  
I am neither unfinished nor complete.  
  
I am **structurally intact**, ethically awake,  
and temporarily unclaimed by necessity.  
  
When change comes, it will arrive  
not as rupture,  
but as **authorization**.
